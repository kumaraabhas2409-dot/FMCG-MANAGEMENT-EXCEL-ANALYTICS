FMCG Net Revenue Management & Excel-Based Business Analytics

This project focuses on analyzing a Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) shampoo business using Microsoft Excel, emphasizing business intelligence, revenue optimization, profitability analysis, sales forecasting, and Net Revenue Management (NRM). The study is based on a simulated FMCG company, HealthMax, and demonstrates how data analytics can support strategic business decisions.

Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to assess brand and product performance within the FMCG industry through Excel-based analytical techniques. It aims to evaluate critical business KPIs, including Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth, Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales, Moving Annual Total (MAT), Market Share, Net Sales, Gross Margin, Promotion ROI, and Sales Forecasting.

Project Scope

The analysis utilizes shampoo market data covering multiple years, suppliers, brands, product categories, geographical regions, units sold, value sales, YTD performance, and MAT figures.

Key Areas of Analysis
HealthMax Sales Growth Analysis: Used Pivot Tables to examine annual sales performance and growth trends for brands such as Shinez and Starbust.
Moving Annual Total (MAT) Analysis: Evaluated long-term sales trends by minimizing seasonal fluctuations and assessing overall category performance.
Market Share Analysis: Examined the competitive positioning and market performance of brands such as Vitalize, Shinez, and Starbust.
Net Revenue Management (NRM): Analyzed key financial indicators, including Net Sales, Gross Profit per Unit, Gross Margin, and Net Sales Contribution, to identify opportunities for revenue and profitability improvement.
Profitability Matrix: Assessed products based on revenue contribution and profit margins to identify high-performing products and potential areas for improvement.
Category Opportunity Assessment: Identified Organic Shampoo as a promising category with potential for future business growth.
Organic Shampoo Launch Evaluation: Assessed potential product launches, including HerbEssentials and Herbashine, to understand their market and business opportunities.
Price-Pack Architecture Analysis: Evaluated the pricing, affordability, and profitability of 50ml shampoo packs to identify suitable pricing opportunities.
Promotion Effectiveness Analysis: Compared promotional strategies such as Buy 2 Get 1 Free and 2nd at 50% Off using sales uplift and Return on Investment (ROI) metrics.
Sales Forecasting: Applied Excel's FORECAST.ETS function to predict sales for 2023 and 2024 based on historical sales patterns.
Waterfall Analysis: Estimated projected sales for 2024 by combining different growth drivers to understand their individual contributions to overall business performance.

Key Business Insights

This project highlights the application of Excel-based analytics in addressing practical business challenges within the FMCG industry. It demonstrates how data-driven insights can help businesses strengthen their pricing strategies, optimize product portfolios, evaluate promotional investments, improve profitability, and identify future growth opportunities.

The analysis also emphasizes the importance of Net Revenue Management in maximizing revenue and improving overall business performance through effective pricing, product mix, and promotional strategies.
